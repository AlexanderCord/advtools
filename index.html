02 — how we work

Audit deep. Plan in detail. Control execution.

01 / Audit deep

Evidence before opinion

Read-only access, the people who actually do the work interviewed rather than only the person who commissioned us, and every finding priced in money at stake per month. Tracking gets verified before any number built on it is trusted.

02 / Plan in detail

Sequenced, costed, executable

Not a strategy deck. A ranked list with remediation as cost ranges, dependencies made explicit, and the first two weeks specified in enough detail to start on Monday — by your team, by your vendor, or by us.

03 / Control execution

Close enough that the plan survives

Plans die on contact with delivery. We stay close enough to catch that — review gates, checkpoints, and someone accountable for the gap between what was agreed and what shipped — without becoming a dependency you cannot remove.

Planned

Fixed scope, fixed fee

Two to three weeks, priced before we start, delivered as a written report plus a walkthrough with whoever will do the work. You keep the document.

Crisis

Surgical cuts

Something is on fire: a launch is slipping, spend is haemorrhaging, a deal is in diligence next week. We find the smallest intervention that stops the bleeding, then write the plan for everything else. Scoped in days, not weeks.
