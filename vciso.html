01 — the trigger

Nobody hires a vCISO because they want one

It is almost always one of four moments, and all four have a deadline attached to someone else's decision.

SALES

An enterprise deal stalled

Procurement sent a 300-row security questionnaire and the honest answers would lose the deal. Someone has to own both the answers and the plan that makes them true.

BOARD

An investor asked

A diligence process, a new board member from a regulated industry, or a cyber insurance renewal that now demands evidence rather than a signed form.

INCIDENT

Something already happened

A breach, a near miss, or a vendor of yours that got hit. The question in the room is no longer whether to fund security but who is accountable for it.

DEPARTURE

The person who knew it all left

Security lived in one engineer's head and that engineer resigned. You need continuity now and a permanent hire in six months, not the reverse.

02 — what it is

A function, not a document

The common failure mode is paying for a policy pack that nobody reads and nothing enforces. What a customer's auditor actually tests is whether the control ran — so the work is operational, month after month.
